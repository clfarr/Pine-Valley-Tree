Our Services

Four generations of expertise serving Weaverville and Trinity County since 1945

Tree Trimming & Pruning

Keep your trees healthy, safe, and looking their best with our professional trimming and pruning services. We carefully assess each tree to determine the best approach for optimal health and aesthetics.

  • Crown thinning and shaping
  • Dead branch removal
  • Clearance pruning for buildings and power lines
  • Seasonal maintenance programs
🪓

Tree Removal

When a tree becomes hazardous, diseased, or needs to be removed for construction, our experienced team handles the job safely and efficiently. We use the latest equipment and techniques to protect your property.

  • Complete tree removal
  • Emergency storm damage removal
  • Stump grinding and removal
  • Lot clearing for construction
  • Crane-assisted removal for difficult situations
🔥

Premium Firewood Sales

Stay warm all winter with our premium seasoned hardwood. We offer a variety of wood types and can cut and split to your specifications. Local delivery available.

  • Seasoned oak, maple, and mixed hardwood
  • Full cords, half cords, and face cords available
  • Cut and split to your preferred size
  • Stacked delivery service
  • Bulk discounts for early season orders

Additional Services

  • Tree health assessments and consultations
  • Cabling and bracing for structural support

  • Brush clearing and land management
  • 24/7 emergency tree services
