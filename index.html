A Legacy of Excellence

1945

Grandpa Roy started Pine Valley Tree Services in Weaverville, CA with just a handsaw, a ladder, and an unshakeable work ethic.

1970s

Second generation takes over, expanding services and modernizing equipment while maintaining Roy's commitment to quality.

2000s

Third generation introduces Safe Space fire protection services, helping protect California homes from wildfire.

Today

Four generations strong, continuing Grandpa Roy's legacy with modern techniques and old-fashioned values.
