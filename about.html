About Pine Valley Tree Services

Family-owned and trusted since 1995

Our Story

Pine Valley Tree Services was founded in 1995 by Jim and Martha Henderson, a husband-and-wife team with a passion for the outdoors and a commitment to their community. What started as a small operation with just a pickup truck and a chainsaw has grown into the area's most trusted tree care service.

For over 30 years, we've been serving our neighbors in Pine Valley and the surrounding areas with honest, quality tree care. We're not a faceless corporation - we're your neighbors, and we take pride in every job we do, whether it's trimming a single tree or clearing an entire property.

Today, the business is run by the Henderson family's son, Mike, who grew up learning the trade and shares his parents' dedication to quality work and fair prices. Our team has expanded over the years, but we've never lost sight of our core values: treat every customer like family, do the job right, and stand behind our work.
