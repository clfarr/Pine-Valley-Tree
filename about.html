About Pine Valley Tree Services

Four generations of family-owned excellence since 1945

The Legacy of Grandpa Roy

In 1945, just as World War II was ending and servicemen were returning home, Roy Thompson started Pine Valley Tree Services in Weaverville, California with nothing but a handsaw, a ladder, and an unshakeable work ethic. Grandpa Roy, as he came to be known throughout Trinity County, understood that in a small mountain town, your word and your work were everything.

What began as one man helping his neighbors care for their trees grew into a beloved local institution. Roy's son picked up a chainsaw alongside his father in the 1960s, learning not just the technical skills of tree care, but the values that made Pine Valley special: honesty, fairness, and treating every customer like family.

By the 1980s, the third generation had joined the family business, and in the 2000s, Grandpa Roy's great-grandchildren began learning the ropes. Today, nearly 80 years after Grandpa Roy climbed his first tree in Weaverville, Pine Valley Tree Services is still family-owned, still based in the same community, and still guided by the same principles Roy established in 1945.

We're not a faceless corporation or a franchise. We're your neighbors. We shop at the same stores, our kids go to the same schools, and we have the same pride in our community that Grandpa Roy had all those years ago. When you call Pine Valley Tree Services, you're not just getting tree care—you're getting four generations of expertise and a family that stands behind every job.
